Workout Intensity Over Time: Energy Change Analysis

Hypothesis

As Active Kilocalories increase on any given day, the energy change will more frequently show a negative result

In addition, longer workout Duration and high Maximum Heart Rate might amplify the overall effort, leading to loss Energy.

Sketch Description

A time series scatterplot with Date/Date on the X-axis and Active Kilocalories on the Y-Axis. Each point's size represents the workout Duration and its color represents the Max Heart Rate. The Shape is used to show the net change in enerrgy: a Triangle if energy went up, an upside down Triangle if it went down, and a cirrle if no change. This allows observation of high intensity clusters over time and their correlation with the change of energy.

Data Encoding

  • X-Axis: Date/Day (Temporal/Sequential)
  • Y-Axis: Active Kilocalories (Quantitative - Intensity)
  • Color: Max Heart Rate in BPM (Sequential Gradient: Low to High Intensity)
  • Shape (Glyph): Energy Change (Post workout - Morning workout)
  • Size: Duration in min (Quantitative)
